# UV
## The Galaxy Evolution Explorer ([GALEX](http://www.galex.caltech.edu/)) satellite 
a NASA mission led by the [California Institute of Technology](http://www.srl.caltech.edu/), 
primary goal was to investigate how star formation in galaxies evolved from the early Universe up to the present.

### Active From

**Launch**:  April 28, 2003

**Observing**: July 7, 2003 - February 7, 2012

### Resolution 

-   1.5" per pixel
-   **Image FWHM**: ~4.2" (FUV) ; ~5.3" (NUV)
-   **Spectral Resolution**: ~200 (FUV) ; ~118 (NUV)
