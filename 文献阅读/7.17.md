Name：
The SAMI Galaxy Survey: The Relationship between Galaxy Rotation and the Motion of Neighbours

Data source：
Sydney-AAO (Australian Astronomical Observatory) Multi-object Integral field spectrograph (SAMI; Croom et al. 2012) as part of the SAMI Galaxy Survey;patially resolved spectroscopy for 3068 galaxies between a redshift of 0.004 and 0.115, with wavelength coverage between 37005700 Å in the blue and 63007400 Å in the red.

Method：
calculate the average line-of-sight velocity offset between a SAMI galaxy and its neighbours
算出相对于某一个星系视线方向的速度，然后分别算左右两侧的bin里的星系的平均值的速度差，如果有相关性，速度差为正，并把星系分类

result：
We find only very small velocity offsets that are significant up to 2sigma level for the neighbouring galaxies within 1 Mpc，2Mpc时比较明显；low-mass, high-mass, early-type
and low-spin galaxies subsamples have stronger signal 

Worth to learn：
结论性：邻近的星系对spin的影响不大，大尺度的影响比较大；
方法性：检测大尺度结构影响的方法可以学习
