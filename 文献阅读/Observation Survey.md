# Optical


#### [MATLAS ](https://matlas.astro.unistra.fr/WP/)_Mass Assembly of early-Type GaLAxies with their fine  Structures_

^cb9131

**Name**: MATLAS
**Telescope**:  Canada-France-Hawaii Telescope
**Target**: Early-Type Galaxies
**Goal**: investigates the mass assembly of Early-Type Galaxies (ETGs) and the build-up of their scaling relations, with extremely deep optical images.
**Advantage**:  1) detect low surface brightness structures and objects, as faint as 29 mag/arcsec2 in the g band. 2)  [ATLAS3D](http://www-astro.physics.ox.ac.uk/atlas3d/) project ancillary dataset:  dynamics, gas and stellar content of the host galaxy
**Content**: deep multi-color images of 1 square degree fields centered on ETGs with distances below 42 Mpc, outside the Virgo cluster; 177 massive lenticular and elliptical galaxies, 55 massive spiral companions, about 2000 dwarf galaxy satellites, and among them about 100 ultra diffuse galaxies.
**Complemented**: the Next Generation Virgo Cluster Survey (NGVS) and Canada France Imaging Survey (CFIS)
**Results**:  [2208.08443](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.08443.pdf)
