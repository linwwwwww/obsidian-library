**对于PA**：
1._We first measure the PA directly from the velocity map as_

_the direction connecting the velocity extrema._

2._– In a similar way, we first identify the PA of the minor axis,_

_and get the major axis from a 90__◦_ _offset._

3._– We fit the velocity and dispersion maps in 2D using_

_DysmalPy, leaving the PA as a free parameter._

4._– We infer the PA from the isophotal fitting of both the NIR_

_continuum light distribution (typically of the JWST F444W_

_filter, or the reddest available filter), and of the NOEMA CO_

_flux data._
